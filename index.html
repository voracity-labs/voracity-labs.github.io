Engineering beyond the prototype

Correctness, security, reliability, and maintainability are prerequisites for serious software, not refinements reserved for later. We draw inspiration from Google’s Site Reliability Engineering practices, which treat availability, latency, capacity, and performance as measurable engineering concerns, and NIST’s Secure Software Development Framework, which integrates security throughout the software development lifecycle.

We approach implementation with the same discipline: explicit assumptions, defensible design decisions, systematic testing, and scrutiny of failure modes. A successful demonstration establishes feasibility; robust engineering establishes utility.

What is AI to us?

We regard artificial intelligence as a powerful engineering discipline, not a universal solution. Google’s 2025 DORA research characterizes AI as an amplifier of existing organizational strengths and dysfunctions, emphasizing that its benefits depend on the environment in which it is deployed.

Independent evidence reinforces the need for empirical evaluation. In a 2025 randomized controlled trial, METR found that early-2025 AI coding tools increased task completion times by 19% for experienced developers working in familiar, mature open-source repositories. The finding is specific to that study, not a universal verdict on AI-assisted development. Its significance lies in the distinction between perceived productivity and measured performance.

We therefore evaluate AI systems by what they demonstrably accomplish, not by their apparent sophistication, benchmark reputation, or adoption alone.
